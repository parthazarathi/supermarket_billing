- Google Drive backup is now one click: Settings > Google Drive > + Connect Google Drive, pick your Google account, authorize - done. No Google Cloud setup, no credential files, nothing to type
- Backups are encrypted on your computer before upload - your data is protected end to end, and Google only ever sees the encrypted file
- Backups now go to a visible "MARTPOS Backups" folder in your Google Drive - no folder creation needed, MartPOS makes it for you
- Automatic backups keep working even when the app is closed or you're away - the MartPOS service handles it securely
- New: Change Account button to switch which Google account receives backups, and Disconnect keeps all your existing backup files
- More reliable restores: downloaded backups are verified before they replace your data, with a safety backup taken first as always
- If Google Drive is unreachable, local backups continue as normal and sales, printing and reports are never affected
- No changes to billing, inventory, customers, reports or your data - same business logic
